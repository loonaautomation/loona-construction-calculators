About Loona Construction Calculators

What this site is

Loona Construction Calculators is a free collection of 12 calculators for building, renovation and DIY: paint, tile, concrete, flooring, drywall (plasterboard), brick, gravel, roof pitch, stairs, board foot, wallpaper and mulch. Every calculator shows its formula and a worked example, so you can check the maths yourself.

Metric and imperial, properly

Most construction calculators are imperial-only. Ours are built metric-first and imperial-complete: flip the unit switch and inputs, standard sizes (1200 × 2400 mm sheets or 4 × 8 ft, European or US wallpaper rolls, 25 kg or 80 lb bags) and results all change system. That is deliberate — Loona builds for Europe and the whole world, not one market.

Part of Loona Automation

This site is one of the free Loona tool sites built by Loona Automation, founded by Aurang Zeb Loona, a school teacher and AI teacher trainer from Jaranwala, Pakistan. Loona's mission — “Bolti Duniya” — is free, genuinely useful tools that solve real problems, with no sign-up and no data leaving your device.

Estimates, not engineering

Results are careful estimates using the standard formulas on each page, with a waste allowance for real-world conditions. They do not replace local building codes, structural engineering or professional advice.
