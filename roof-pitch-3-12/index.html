3/12 Roof Pitch — 14.0°

a 3/12 pitch is 14.04°, grade 25%, rafter multiplier 1.0308 — with rafter tables and material guidance. Everything for one pitch: angle, grade, the rafter multiplier, lengths for common runs, and honest notes on what roofing belongs on a 3/12 slope.

3/12 pitch at a glance

Pitch3 in 12 (rise 3 units per 12 units of run)
Slope angle14.04°
Grade25.0%
Rafter multiplier1.0308 — multiply the horizontal run by this to get rafter (slope) length
Rafter length per 12 ft run12.37 ft
Typical usesRanch houses, porches, garages in mild climates

Slope length only — add eaves overhang and ridge allowance when ordering timber, exactly as the Roof Pitch Calculator notes.

Working with a 3/12 pitch

Low pitch. Shingles are allowed by most codes at 3/12 with enhanced underlayment; snow slides slowly and ice dams are a real risk in cold climates.

Worked example

A garage roof spans 24 ft, so each side runs 12 ft horizontally. At 3/12 the ridge rises 3 ft above the wall plate, and each rafter is 12 × 1.0308 = 12.37 ft of slope length before overhangs. Roofing material is ordered on slope area: the 24 × 30 ft building (two 12-ft runs, 30 ft long) needs 2 × 12.37 × 30 ≈ 742 sq ft of covering — not the 720 sq ft of the floor plan. That multiplier gap is where DIY budgets go wrong.

Frequently Asked Questions

What angle is a 3/12 pitch?

14.04 degrees from horizontal — a 25% grade.

How long is a rafter at 3/12?

Run × 1.0308. A 12 ft run needs 12.37 ft of rafter before overhang and ridge allowances.

Is 3/12 walkable?

Yes — pitches up to about 6/12 are generally considered walkable with care and proper footwear; this describes common practice, not a safety guarantee.

What roofing suits 3/12?

Low pitch.
