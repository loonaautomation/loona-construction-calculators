Frequently Asked Questions

Can I skip primer?

On sound walls in a similar color, usually yes. New plaster, patches and bold color changes need it — the table includes it so the decision is yours with numbers attached.

Walls only — no ceiling?

Subtract the ceiling row: walls alone need 8.4 L for two coats.

What nap roller?

3/8 in for smooth walls (most interiors); 1/2 in for textured or exterior surfaces.

How long will it take?

One room, two coats with drying time: a realistic weekend day — prep and taping are half the work, which is why they're on the list.
