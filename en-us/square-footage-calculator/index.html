📐 Loona Square Footage Calculator

Work out the area of one room or a whole home. Add as many rooms and spaces as you need — rectangles, circles, triangles and L-shapes — in feet & inches or meters, and get a running total in square feet, square meters, square yards and acres, with an optional cost estimate.

How to use the Loona Square Footage Calculator

  1. Choose your unit system — imperial (feet & inches) or metric (meters).
  2. For each room, pick its shape: rectangle, circle, triangle, or L-shape (entered as two rectangles).
  3. Enter the measurements — imperial dimensions accept feet and inches separately, e.g. 10 ft 6 in.
  4. Add more rooms with the button; every room shows its own area and the total updates live.
  5. Optionally enter a price per square foot or per square meter to get a total cost estimate.
Example: A living room 12 ft × 10 ft is 120 sq ft. An L-shaped kitchen made of a 10 ft × 8 ft rectangle plus a 4 ft × 6 ft rectangle is 80 + 24 = 104 sq ft. Together: 224 sq ft = 20.81 sq m = 24.89 sq yd = 0.0051 acres.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I measure an L-shaped room?

Split the L into two rectangles. Measure the first rectangle (length × width), then the second rectangle that completes the shape, and add the two areas together. In this calculator, choose the L-Shape option for that room — it asks for Rectangle A and Rectangle B and adds them for you, so there is no corner to subtract and no double-counting.

How do I convert square feet to square meters?

Divide square feet by 10.7639. For example, 120 sq ft ÷ 10.7639 = 11.15 sq m. To go the other way, multiply square meters by 10.7639. This calculator shows both (plus square yards and acres) for every room and for the total, so you never need to convert by hand.

What is an acre in square feet?

One acre is exactly 43,560 square feet — about 4,047 square meters, or a square roughly 209 ft × 209 ft. A quarter-acre plot is 10,890 sq ft. The calculator shows acres to 4 decimal places, which is handy for plots, gardens and land as well as rooms.

How it works — the formulas

Every shape is worked out in square feet first, then converted exactly: 1 sq m = 10.7639 sq ft, 1 sq yd = 9 sq ft and 1 acre = 43,560 sq ft. Metric measurements are converted from meters to feet (1 m = 1 ÷ 0.3048 ft) before the same formulas run, so both systems give the same answer for the same room.

Rectangle = Length × Width
Circle = π × Radius² (radius is half the diameter)
Triangle = Base × Height ÷ 2
L-Shape = (Length A × Width A) + (Length B × Width B) — the L is split into two rectangles that meet without overlapping, so nothing is counted twice and no corner needs subtracting.

Worked example

Example: Rectangle 10 ft × 12 ft = 120 sq ft = 120 ÷ 10.7639 = 11.15 sq m = 120 ÷ 9 = 13.33 sq yd = 120 ÷ 43,560 = 0.0028 acres. A circle with radius 5 ft is π × 25 = 78.54 sq ft, and a triangle with base 10 ft and height 8 ft is 10 × 8 ÷ 2 = 40 sq ft.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure an irregularly shaped room?

Break it into simple shapes you can measure: rectangles, triangles and circles (or part-circles). Add one room entry per piece in this calculator, pick the matching shape for each, and the running total adds them all up. Sketch the floor plan first and write each measurement on the sketch — it prevents missed corners.

Can I use the total for flooring, tiles or paint?

Yes — the total area is exactly the starting number those jobs need. Use it in the Flooring Calculator or Tile Calculator to get boxes and tiles with waste allowed for, the Carpet Calculator for carpet, and the Paint Calculator for wall paint (that one works from wall area, so enter the room dimensions there). Add 5–10% extra when ordering materials for cuts and mistakes.
