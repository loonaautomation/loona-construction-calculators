Brick Size in Germany (NF) — 240 × 115 × 71 mm

Germany (NF)'s standard brick is 240×115×71 mm — 49.4 bricks per m² single-skin with 10 mm joints. The complete card for the German Normalformat (NF): dimensions in metric and imperial, bricks per square meter and per square foot, and what 1,000 bricks will build.

German Normalformat (NF) — the numbers

Brick size (L × W × H)240 × 115 × 71 mm (9.45 × 4.53 × 2.80 in)
Mortar joint assumed10 mm
Size incl. one joint250 × 81 mm (the laying module)
Bricks per m² (single-skin)49.4
Bricks per sq ft4.59
Wall covered by 1,000 bricks20.2 m² (218 sq ft) single-skin
Double-skin wall99 per m² — double the single-skin count

The Normalformat (240 × 115 × 71) is the reference size of German masonry, coordinated to the 250 mm planning module.

Worked example — a 10 × 10 ft wall

A 10 × 10 ft (100 sq ft) single-skin wall in Germany (NF): 100 × 4.59 = 459 bricks, and with the customary 5% for breakages and cuts, 482 bricks to order. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard brick size in Germany (NF)?

240 × 115 × 71 mm (german normalformat (nf)). With a 10 mm joint it lays at 49.4 bricks per m² of single-skin wall.

Why do counts differ between countries?

Because the brick itself differs — a longer, taller brick covers more wall. The laying module (brick + one joint) in each direction decides the count: count = 1 ÷ (module length × module height).

Does mortar joint thickness matter?

Yes: at 12 mm instead of 10 mm the count drops by roughly 3%. Sloppy thick joints quietly eat bricks — and mortar.

Single-skin or double-skin?

The per-m² figure is one brick wide (half-brick wall). Structural/load-bearing walls are usually double-skin: double every number.
