Frequently Asked Questions

How do I measure my roof's pitch?

From the attic or a ladder: mark 12 in horizontally on a level, measure the vertical rise to the roof surface at that point. That rise over 12 is your pitch.

Which pitch is most common?

4/12 to 6/12 covers most North American housing — the middle rows of this chart.
