🧱 Drywall Calculator

Sheeting a room with plasterboard or drywall? Enter the room dimensions and the calculator works out the wall area (plus ceiling, if you are boarding it) and how many standard sheets you need — 1200 × 2400 mm sheets in metric, 4 × 8 ft sheets in imperial.

How to use the Drywall Calculator

  1. Choose metric or imperial units.
  2. Enter the room length, width and wall height.
  3. Tick the ceiling box if you are boarding the ceiling too.
  4. Pick your sheet size (1200 × 2400 mm or 4 × 8 ft are standard).
  5. Press Calculate for the sheet count, waste included.
Example: A 4 m × 3.5 m room with 2.7 m walls and the ceiling boarded has 54.5 m² to cover — 21 standard 1200 × 2400 mm sheets including 10% waste.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many plasterboard sheets do I need for a room?

Work out the wall area (perimeter × height), add the ceiling if boarding it, add 10% for cuts, and divide by the sheet area — 2.88 m² for a 1200 × 2400 mm sheet or 32 ft² for a 4 × 8 ft sheet.

Should I subtract doors and windows?

No. The board around doors and windows is cut from full sheets and the offcuts are rarely reusable elsewhere; the waste allowance covers it. Subtracting openings usually leaves you a sheet short.

Which sheet size should I choose?

1200 × 2400 mm (metric) and 4 × 8 ft (imperial) are the standard, cheapest sheets and fit standard stud spacing. Longer 2700 mm / 12 ft sheets suit tall rooms and reduce joints, but are harder to handle alone.

How it works — the formula

Area to board = 2 × (Length + Width) × Height, plus Length × Width if the ceiling is included
Sheets = Area × (1 + Waste %) ÷ Sheet area, rounded up

Sheet areas: 1200 × 2400 mm = 2.88 m²; 1200 × 2700 mm = 3.24 m²; 4 × 8 ft = 32 ft² (2.97 m²); 4 × 12 ft = 48 ft² (4.46 m²).

Worked example

Example: Room 4 m × 3.5 m, height 2.7 m, ceiling included: walls = 2 × 7.5 × 2.7 = 40.5 m², ceiling = 14 m² → 54.5 m². With 10% waste: 59.95 m² ÷ 2.88 m² per sheet = 20.8 → 21 sheets.

Frequently asked questions

What thickness of drywall do I need?

12.5 mm (½ in) is standard for walls and ceilings. Use moisture-resistant board in bathrooms, fire-rated board where regulations require it (garages, party walls), and 9.5 mm (⅜ in) only for curved work.

How much waste should I allow?

10% for rectangular rooms. Stairwells, sloped ceilings and rooms with many openings justify 15%. Sheets damaged in transport are replaced by the merchant — check them on delivery.

Can I calculate in feet?

Yes — switch to Imperial for feet and 4 × 8 or 4 × 12 ft sheets. Because metric and imperial sheet sizes differ slightly, the count can differ by a sheet for the same room; buy for the sheets you will actually purchase.
