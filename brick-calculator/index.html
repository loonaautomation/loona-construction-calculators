🧱 Brick Calculator

Building a wall, extension or garden feature? Enter the wall length and height and choose your brick — standard metric brick with 10 mm mortar joints, or US modular brick with ⅜-inch joints — and get the brick count for a single-skin (half-brick) wall, waste included.

How to use the Brick Calculator

  1. Choose metric or imperial units for the wall dimensions.
  2. Enter the wall length and height.
  3. Choose the brick type — the count per m² or ft² includes mortar joints.
  4. Press Calculate. Counts are for a single-skin wall; double them for double-skin.
Example: A garden wall 5 m long and 2.6 m high (13 m²) in standard brick needs 848 bricks including 10% extra.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bricks are in a square metre?

About 60 standard bricks (215 × 65 mm) per m² of single-skin wall including 10 mm mortar joints — 59.3 before waste. A double-skin wall needs about 120 per m².

How many bricks per square foot?

US modular bricks: about 6.86 per ft² of single-skin wall with ⅜ in joints. US standard (8 in) bricks: about 6.55 per ft². Both figures include the mortar joint.

Should I subtract windows and doors?

For one or two small openings, no — the bricks around openings involve cuts and the waste allowance absorbs them. For large openings (garage doors, big windows), subtract their area from the wall before calculating.

How it works — the formula

Bricks = Wall area × Bricks per unit area × (1 + Waste %)

Bricks per unit area come from the brick face plus one mortar joint: a standard 215 × 65 mm brick with a 10 mm joint occupies 225 × 75 mm, giving 59.3 bricks per m². A US modular brick (7⅞ × 2¼ in) with a ⅞ in joint occupies 8 × 2.625 in, giving 6.86 bricks per ft²; a US standard 8 in brick gives 6.55 per ft².

Worked example

Example: Wall 5 m × 2.6 m = 13 m². Standard bricks: 13 × 59.26 = 770.4 bricks; with 10% extra: 847.4 → 848 bricks for a single-skin wall (1,696 for double-skin).

Frequently asked questions

What about mortar — how much do I need?

As a rough guide, a single-skin wall in standard brick uses about 0.03 m³ of mortar per m² of wall (roughly 25–30 kg of dry mix per m²). Mortar depends heavily on joint thickness and brick absorption, so treat this as an estimate.

Why the waste allowance?

Bricks get cut at ends and openings, some arrive chipped, and a few break in handling. 10% is standard; keep leftovers for future repairs — reclaimed matching bricks are hard to find later.

Does it work for blocks too?

The same method applies: a standard 440 × 215 mm block with a 10 mm joint works out at about 9.9 blocks per m². Enter your wall, choose the standard brick count as a rough guide only — for blocks, divide the wall area by 0.099 m² per block instead.
