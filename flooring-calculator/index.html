🪵 Flooring Calculator

Laminate, vinyl plank or engineered wood — enter the room size and the coverage printed on the box, and this calculator tells you how many boxes to buy, cutting waste included. Works in square metres or square feet.

How to use the Flooring Calculator

  1. Choose metric or imperial units.
  2. Enter the room length and width (measure the longest and widest points).
  3. Enter the coverage per box from the product packaging.
  4. Set the cutting-waste allowance — 10% for straight lay, 15% for diagonal.
  5. Press Calculate for the number of boxes.
Example: A 5 m × 4 m room (20 m²) needs 22 m² of flooring with 10% waste — exactly 10 boxes when each box covers 2.2 m².

Frequently Asked Questions

How much waste should I allow for laminate flooring?

10% for a straight (parallel) layout in a rectangular room. Allow 15% for diagonal or herringbone layouts, and 15–20% for rooms with many alcoves, bays or angled walls.

Where do I find the coverage per box?

On the packaging and in the product listing — it is the total area the planks in one box cover, for example 2.2 m² or 24 ft². It differs between brands, plank widths and ranges, so never guess it.

Should I buy one extra box?

Yes — keep one unopened box from the same batch. Flooring ranges are discontinued regularly, and a spare box makes a future repair possible instead of a full re-floor.

How it works — the formula

Flooring needed = Room length × Room width × (1 + Waste %)
Boxes = Flooring needed ÷ Coverage per box, rounded up

Irregular rooms: split them into rectangles, calculate each, and add the boxes together. Measure skirting-to-skirting, and into doorways and alcoves.

Worked example

Example: Room 5 m × 4 m = 20 m². With 10% cutting waste: 22 m². Boxes covering 2.2 m² each: 22 ÷ 2.2 = 10 boxes exactly.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure an L-shaped room?

Split it into two rectangles, measure each, and either add the areas before calculating or calculate boxes per rectangle and add them. Always measure to the skirting board, not the wall behind it.

Does this work for vinyl and engineered wood too?

Yes — any flooring sold by coverage per box or pack: laminate, LVT and vinyl plank, engineered and solid wood boards, and decking boards when sold by coverage.

Can I use feet and square feet?

Yes — switch to Imperial. Room dimensions in feet, box coverage in ft² per box; the box count matches the metric calculation for the same room.
