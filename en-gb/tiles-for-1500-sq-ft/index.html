Frequently Asked Questions

How many 60×60 tiles cover 1500 sq ft?

422 tiles including 10% extra (106 boxes of 4). Each tile on its 3 mm pitch covers 3.91 sq ft.

Why count 'on pitch'?

Tiles are laid with grout gaps, so each tile effectively occupies (60 + 0.3) cm in each direction. Ignoring the gap under-orders by about 1%.

What about skirting and cuts?

The 10% extra covers standard cuts and breakage. Rooms with many corners, diagonal patterns or feature borders need 15% — change it in the Tile Calculator.

Different tile size?

Counts scale with tile area: halve the tile (30×60) and you roughly double the count. The Tile Calculator takes any size and gap.
